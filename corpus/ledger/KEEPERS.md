# The keepers

Generated by `node scripts/corpus.mjs views` from each recipe's `keepers` under `corpus/recipes/`, and never edited by hand. A feature of greenline is prose plus its keeper: each promise a roster skill's pages state is listed with the keeper that holds it, a fact the CLI reads and prints, a hold, a record the owner can see afterwards or an oracle that tests what stays judgment, or with its gap. An entry is named by its skill and its place in the recipe's list, counted from one, as a walk's Held by lane names it.

## The counts

- Promises: 528, across 47 skills; 0 skills promise nothing.
- Kept: 304, by kind fact 94, hold 102, record 18, oracle 90; by cost free 214, cheap 90, dear 0.
- Gaps: 224, of which 4 warrant a mechanism and 220 stay prose, each tested by its oracle where one exists.
- Hook rules the holds name: 28, `atlas-refresh`, `branch-delete-words`, `card-label`, `change-trailer`, `deal-words`, `design-lock`, `discard-words`, `discussion-words`, `done-checks`, `force-push-words`, `frame-confirmed`, `interface-captures`, `interface-fact`, `kill-by-name`, `landing-words`, `manifest-files`, `marks-by-hand`, `message-trailer`, `no-open-change`, `one-trailer`, `other-change-paths`, `owned-words`, `owner-yes`, `rebase-words`, `record-form`, `resume-open`, `standing-choice` and `visuals-by-hand`.

# Prose: the method

## The answer's evidence

A known name, path, definition or explanation is answered with that answer itself and the repository evidence behind it, with no yes/no prefix and no invented uncertainty. The process is not the answer: report what is true, not the tools you are about to run, unless the owner asked how you work. Where a claim enters a record, the measured number goes in with it. Never "should work", "generally" or "in most cases" where you have not checked. When the owner contradicts an answer you already gave, the reply is a fresh measurement, never the same sentence again: re-run the check, recorded where a change is open, quote the new outcome, and either name the benign difference that explains their result or correct the record plainly.

## When a reply did not land

When the owner says a reply did not land ("wait, I don't understand where you've got to"), re-pitch it rather than repeat it. Give a little context first: where the work stands and what led there. Then say the point in simplified technical English, one thought per sentence with its articles and its verbs. Use the domain's words as `GLOSSARY.md` defines them, following `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` to the right glossary where the repository has more than one.

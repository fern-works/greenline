# To questionnaire

to-questionnaire is a trial habit, installed only where the workspace's `trials` names it, for the moment a decision the work needs can be answered only by someone other than the owner, at whatever step the work is in, most often frame. It writes no record of its own: the questionnaire is a working file, never committed, that the owner hands on, and the answers that come back settle the decision in the section of the step that asked. The file lives in the open change's working files; with no change open, give the questionnaire in the reply instead of a file. A question the owner can answer is frame's to ask; this habit asks the owner only about the send.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the questionnaire is written, read [method.md](method.md): the send, its recipient, what you need back and the document's structure.

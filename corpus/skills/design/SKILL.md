# Design

Design settles how a change's interface looks and behaves before the plan: the direction the owner picks, written where the next change reads it. It runs only where the change's record says the change touches an interface; on any other change its section is one line and you do not load this skill. You propose a direction, a shell or a waiver, and the owner sets it.

Before you read the lock, read [method.md](method.md): the lock and the fact, the one ask, the deal, the prototype, the lock written, and the pages by moment.

## Handoff

Consumes: the change record's frame section and its interface fact; DESIGN.md and the interface rulings where they stand
Produces: the change record's design section at the mode's depth, and the lock the owner picked written into DESIGN.md and the rulings
Next: the next step of the change's mode

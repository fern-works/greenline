# Direction

Read this page when nothing in the home locks a direction for the root the change touches, or the owner asks for a new one. A direction is three things: a **material** (type, palette, surfaces and texture), a **composition** (the first screen's posture, the sections' shapes and the motion's register) and an **anatomy**, the regions the page holds, drawn from the job the page does. The material never decides what the page holds or in what order; the job does.

## Read before you ask

1. Read DESIGN.md nearest the files and the interface rulings. Where they lock a direction for the root, work inside it and stop here: the lock is the owner's.
2. Take stock of the material in hand: the owner's own pictures, real copy, a brand kit, a site they admire, and what is missing. Say what is absent; a direction that needs product photographs is never dealt to a product that has none.
3. Ground the work in the product's own world before any visual choice: list eight to twelve things only this owner has, name five concepts and five colours that occur naturally in that world, one signature element only this product could carry, and three obvious defaults to avoid. Then write the page as claims, each with its proof. With the product's name removed, a reader should still tell what the page is for.

## Ask once

Ask the owner once, in one round, through cards where they render:

- who the page is for;
- the one action it exists for;
- the tone, as an extreme pair the owner leans toward (quiet or loud, warm or exact, editorial or instrumental);
- the material in hand.

Infer the rest and say what you inferred. Ask a further question only where an answer would change the result, at most two; otherwise state the assumption and go on. Never ask again what the round settled.

## Deal three

Deal exactly three directions. Each pair differs on at least four of these eight axes: composition, measure, palette, typography, geometry, surface grammar, motif and motion, and the three differ in the first screen's silhouette, never in colour or labels alone. For each direction:

- write one idea sentence, the subject's own thing becoming a device on the page, and check it against the nearest competitor's page;
- name its material, its composition (editorial, instrumental or a hybrid, one dominant gesture, a grid and one signal colour with a fixed role) and its anatomy from the page's job;
- add one artefact native to the product, such as a real demo or a data specimen, in place of decoration;
- say in a line what it gives up.

With the catalog installed, the deal draws the three by the facets in play and the page's job; without it, compose three yourself from the material and the job. Spend boldness in one place per direction, and keep each coherent: settle its whole first, then derive its tokens from it.

## Pick, reroll, custom

The owner picks with one line saying why. Record a short verdict on each direction not picked and carry its strong parts into the pick. The owner may reroll once, for a reason you can state, such as a clash with the brand or with how the product is used; a second reroll is a custom answer. A custom answer is written in the same three parts, material, composition and anatomy. Before building, check the pick against the brief as a hypothesis: the change, the outcome expected and why.

## A reference or a brand kit

A site the owner brings is studied, never dealt: read [study.md](study.md), keep five to eight references at most, and record what each takes, leaves and becomes. A brand kit is imported into the home: keep its palette and type exactly, map its colours onto the direction's roles while keeping the direction's colour budget, convert values by code and never by eye, and rank measured colours by how often they appear. A brand pair that fails contrast goes only where the floor allows it, such as large text and fills.

## Write the pick

The pick is written into the design section with its catalog entry and pin where the catalog dealt it, and, for a new system, into the home as [home.md](home.md) says: DESIGN.md's tokens and sections, and the rulings' direction line with the date.

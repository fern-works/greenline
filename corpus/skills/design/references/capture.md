# Capture

Read this page at verify, on a change whose record says it touches an interface. The interface is checked as it renders, and the captures are kept as the change's evidence, without an offer.

## The widths and the variants

- Capture each changed page at 1440, 768 and 390 wide, at heights of 900, 1024 and 844, the first screen and the full page, and look at every one.
- The reflow and clipping floors run once more at 320 wide; the captures stay at three widths.
- Capture with reduced motion as well; where the home declares a dark scheme, capture it too.
- Wait for the fonts and the images and keep the settled state; use one fresh load per width; never click anything that writes shared data.
- Capture the app at its own address, never the viewer's. Where a capture cannot be made, the check keeps its limit with the cause; name what was not captured in the record's `## Interface` section, `- limit <route>: <your words>`, so the owner sees it, and read the page in the owner's browser where one shows it. A limit settles the route only with that line beside it.

## Worst-case data

Map every value the page renders with its source and its limit. Build one plausible worst-case fixture that enters where the data does, with long names, long numbers and missing values spread through the first rows, and capture the empty, the one and the huge (a thousand rows or more) states as well. Grade each break as broken, ugly or fragile, list what held, and keep the fixture with the change.

## What to sample

The main task's page, a detail page and a list page, one form flow, one overlay, a loading, empty or error state, and the primary and a contrasting input: pointer and touch, small and large. Say what you left out.

## The kept check

On a redesign, run the before's inventory again on the same pages and widths, with the same command, and compare it with the kept list: every link, field, handler and heading the list names is still there, or its loss is recorded with a reason. A missing entry fails the check.

## Rounds

At most three rounds: read the captures, fix the three findings that matter most, capture again. Stop early when no stranger would notice a problem, and report a weak result plainly rather than polish around it.

## Results

Each item reads pass, fail, not applicable or unverified, and a result is never inferred from another device. A static finding is a hypothesis until a capture or a measurement shows it. Record each measurement's conditions: the viewport, the network, the processor and the cache.

## Native lanes

A native screen is captured from a simulator where the harness has one: on Apple platforms with larger text, dark mode, reduced motion and the accessibility inspector; on Android with large font scale, the dark theme, TalkBack and the window sizes. Where no simulator is reachable, the record says the floor was checked statically and the reviewer read the code, and each screen's run records that browser limit, as the references page says.

## Kept as visuals

Each capture is kept as a visual of the form `capture` in the visual home, with its route, width, commit and scores in its manifest, so the change page shows before and after and drift follows the files as it does for every visual. No browser bar or device frame is drawn, and the sample data holds nothing personal.

# Design: the method

1. **Read the lock and the fact.** Read the home first: the DESIGN.md nearest the files the change touches, and the interface rulings of the decisions book. Where they lock a direction for the root, work inside it and deal nothing; the rest of this page is for what the lock leaves open. Name the components the change builds on the section's `components:` line, from the closed list, the files' names as your hint.
2. **Ask once.** Where nothing locks the direction, ask the owner once, in one round, through cards where they render: who the page is for, the one action it exists for, the tone as an extreme, and the material already in hand, such as a brand kit, real copy, photographs or a site they admire. Do not ask again what those answers settled.
3. **Deal or compose.** A direction is a material, a composition and the anatomy the page's job gives. With the catalog installed, deal three that differ; without it, compose three from the material in hand and the job. A reference the owner brings is studied, never dealt; a brand kit is imported into the home.
4. **Show it.** Deal the three on cards where they render, one per direction with its name and a line, the one reroll an option there; the owner's own direction is their answer on a card, never one you compose on a delegation. Where none render, show the deal view, a specimen or a reply table. Say what each gives up.
5. **Prototype when offered.** Where the open question is the shape itself, offer a prototype on the real route; on a yes, build it by [references/prototype.md](references/prototype.md).
6. **Write the lock.** The owner's pick is the lock: write its tokens and sections into DESIGN.md and its pin into the rulings, and cite both in the section.

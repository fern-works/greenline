# Idea Refine

## Overview

Refines raw ideas into sharp, actionable concepts worth building through structured divergent and convergent thinking.

idea-refine is a trial start for an idea the owner brings that is still vague, before any change. It runs as a conversation in the reply, edits no file, and needs no mode and no record. A direction the owner takes up becomes a change like any other: propose its mode and wait, as AGENTS.md says, open its record with a `## start` section carrying the one-pager's problem statement, direction, assumptions and not-doing list, and run the line from `frame`.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the first question, read [method.md](method.md): when to use it, the three phases, the one-pager and the anti-patterns.

## Handoff

Consumes: the owner's idea, in the request's own words; the repository, when the idea sits inside one
Produces: the one-pager in the reply: the problem statement, the recommended direction, the key assumptions, the MVP scope and the not-doing list; nothing durable until the owner's yes
Next: frame, under the mode the owner approves, for a direction the owner takes up

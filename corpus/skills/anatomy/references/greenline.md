# anatomy: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where anatomy's work lands in the workspace, which `SKILL.md` names by its nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

A figure made to explain a mechanism of the code is a durable visual: its page, `.greenline/visuals/<id>/<slug>.html`, stands under the change with its manifest `<slug>.visual.json` beside it in the form `.greenline/WORK.md` gives ("Durable visuals"), its `skill` `anatomy` and its `form` `figure`; its brief, written before it is built at `.greenline/tmp/<id>/pictures/<slug>.brief.md`, one line a field, is read into the manifest's `brief` by `greenline picture keep`, and `greenline status` names a kept figure without one (GL0606). The keep takes the page alone, and the hooks refuse every other file in the change's folder of the home: its build script and its model module are the record's, copied into the change's ledger folder, `.greenline/ledger/<id>/sources/<slug>/`. A figure for the project's docs or site is a product file: the change writes its script, its model and its page where the owner places them. Either way the record's build section names them. Captures, the ringed PNG of the line check and the audit's output go under the change's working files, `.greenline/tmp/<id>/anatomy/`, never the host's temporary directory and never a `shots/` folder in the repository: `capture.mjs`, `print.mjs` and `lines.mjs` take the path with their `--out` flag, `drive.mjs` takes it in each action's file name, and the audit's report is its standard output, redirected there. What a verdict relies on is promoted to `.greenline/ledger/<id>/verify/<claim-slug>/` before the verify section cites it, or at the independent depth the verifier's report, as verify's references page says.

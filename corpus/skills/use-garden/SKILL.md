# Use garden

garden is a separately installed command that answers engineering guidance
questions from a published body of units. The commands, their flags and their replies
are in [operations.md](operations.md). This habit writes no record of its own: the CLI
writes each consultation's receipts, and the section of the step that made the
choice says what a consultation changed.

Before the first consultation, read [method.md](method.md): when one is relevant, what must be in place, how it runs, what a result is worth here and what to do when it fails.

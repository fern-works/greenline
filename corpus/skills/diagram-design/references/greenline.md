# Diagram design: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where diagram-design's work lands in the workspace and the commands it runs, which `SKILL.md` names by their nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

A diagram is drawn under the change's working files, `.greenline/tmp/<id>/`, and kept with `greenline picture keep <file> --change <id>`, which writes it into the change's folder of the visual home, `.greenline/visuals/<id>/`, a plan's under its planning change's id, such as `.greenline/visuals/2026-10-08-retry-deadline/flow.html`, with its manifest `flow.visual.json` beside it in the form `.greenline/WORK.md` gives ("Durable visuals"), its `skill` `diagram-design` and its `form` the type drawn. The project's style guide is `.greenline/visuals/style-guide.md`; the installed `references/style-guide.md` is the one `greenline sync` owns and never edited.

## The skill's brief

A fresh agent, started by the change's build or its close with the skill's brief, makes the map, each atlas page and its journey and every durable diagram; the session draws only inline. show-me's table of kinds says which diagram fits which shape, the change in the whole, the plan's and the decision's among them. The reply offers a diagram at most once per change, where the shape a turn wants passes show-me's caps, in the line `Picture offered: <what it would show>, <form>, <finish>; say keep`, which the hooks count, and never a lost diagram's remake. On the owner's ask a fresh agent draws it, at every mode, quick included, since an agent a method starts stands outside the mode's count of agents, never the session itself, with the work guide's brief and nothing else: what it shows and for whom, its type, the change's id, `git rev-parse HEAD` as its commit, the files it covers, and the file it draws under `.greenline/tmp/<id>/` for `greenline picture keep`; and the diagram's brief the session wrote first, `.greenline/tmp/<id>/pictures/<slug>.brief.md`, one line a field, `- question:`, `- form:`, `- nodes:`, `- finish:`, `- palette:` and `- reader:`, which `greenline picture keep` reads into the manifest's `brief`; `greenline status` names a kept diagram without one (GL0606). The reply that follows the making gives the owner the address the viewer serves for it, and the record links the change's visuals once, as `[visuals](<id>/visuals.json)`.

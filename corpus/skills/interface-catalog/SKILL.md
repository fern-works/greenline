# Interface catalog

interface-catalog is an extra habit, installed on the owner's request, for the moment the design step deals directions: a change whose record says it touches an interface, where nothing locked a direction for its root. It writes no record of its own; the deal and the owner's pick are written in the change's design section. The design step owns the ask, the showing and the lock, and the interface rules own every constraint a measurement can check. The deal never prints the catalog whole.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the deal is drawn, read [method.md](method.md): how the deal is drawn, reading an entry, and what follows the pick.

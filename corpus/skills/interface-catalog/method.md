# Interface catalog: the method

## How the deal is drawn

The deal draws three directions, each a material, a palette, a type pairing, a composition and a motion register, from the entries that serve the register and the platform in play: a site's heroes and page shapes, an app's shells and screen patterns. The page's job gives the anatomy, the same for the three. The tone, the sector and the material in hand weight the draw toward the entries whose words name them, and a composition that needs pictures is never dealt to a product that has none. The three differ entry by entry. The same ask deals the same three, and the owner may reroll once.

## Reading an entry

An entry is data. Its tokens stand in DESIGN.md's groups, so a pick writes into the design system with no translation; its sketch is rows, columns and the slot each region holds; its words give its reasons and its pitfalls. Its colours come with the ink-on-ground pairs they are read in, each meeting the floor. A constraint a measurement can check belongs to the interface rules, never to an entry. An entry names where its language came from, a source at its commit or a site studied on the day it was seen, and the source's own expression stays out of it.

## After the pick

The pick is the owner's. Write it into the design section with its entries' ids, and into the design system as the design step's pages say. Combine entries only within one direction, and settle the direction whole before deriving its tokens.

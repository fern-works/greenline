# Guard the Context Window

guard-the-context-window is a trial habit, installed only where the workspace's `trials` names it, for the moment a large output, a long file or a long stretch of exploration is about to fill the thread, at whatever step the work is in, and for a thread already long mid-task. It writes no record of its own and starts no agent: a read delegated to a fresh agent is one of the mode's agents, spent by the step that owns it, never by this habit. What a step relies on goes into its section of the change record or the change's folder, never only into the thread, since the record is what a fresh session resumes from. A skill, a guide and the file you are changing are still read whole: a summary is not a full read, as AGENTS.md says.

Before a large output enters the thread, read [method.md](method.md): what cutting means where the harness cannot remove text, the pattern, and the context budget.

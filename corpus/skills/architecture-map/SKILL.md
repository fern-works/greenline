# Architecture map

architecture-map is the habit, a default, for the owner who wants to see, onboard into or refresh how the code fits together, and for the atlas's map. The map is made only on the owner's ask, nothing offered; show-me's table of kinds says where the map fits. It writes no record of its own: the map is the repository's state, and the section of the change that writes it names the map.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the map is built or refreshed, read [method.md](method.md): its files, its fresh agent and its refresh, the one rule, the six steps, updating a map, scale and what not to do.

Durable output: .greenline/visuals/map/ (map.md, snapshot.json, architecture.html and its manifest architecture.visual.json), made by a fresh agent on the owner's ask, refreshed by the close without asking when a page its change touched changed; a larger refresh waits for the owner's ask.

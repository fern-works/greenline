# Frame

Frame turns a request into a shared understanding of what is wanted before any plan or code exists: the intent, what is out of scope, the terms, and the questions still open. It also runs mid-work, at any step, the moment scope, intent or a term turns out unclear: that ambiguity is put to the owner at the inline depth, never guessed through. Facts are yours to look up first, decisions the owner's.

A request naming its mode, or one `proceed` allows, starts at once: one line states the mode and why, no yes awaited.

Before the first question read [method.md](method.md): the intent, the nodes, what to read first and what work settles without asking. When the proposal is written, read [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md), "The proposal".

## Handoff

Consumes: the request and the change record's front matter (its mode and why); at the written depth, the repository's decision home, its glossary and the records of earlier changes in the same area; the research reports of the topics the owner accepted
Produces: the change record's frame section in .greenline/ledger/<id>.md, one line at the inline depth, and at the written depth its questions in the list form, the confirmed restatement, its out-of-scope list, the settled terms and any open question; the reply alone when no change is open
Next: the next step of the change's mode

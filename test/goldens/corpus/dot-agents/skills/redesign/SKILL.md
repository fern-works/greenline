# Redesign

A redesign changes how a working interface looks without breaking what it does. Start it before any edit when the owner asks for a page, a screen or a whole site to be redesigned, or for a working page to look different while it keeps what it does, such as denser, cleaner or like another product; it captures what stands, writes down what must survive, states the constraint, and hands the work on at careful, where the design step and two reviews carry it.

1. **Name the depth.** A polish keeps the system and fixes its details; a restyle replaces the material and keeps the structure; a full redesign replaces both. An audit alone writes findings and changes nothing.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md).

Before the first capture, read [method.md](method.md): the before, the kept list, the audit, the constraint and the hand-on at careful.

## Handoff

Consumes: the owner's request to redesign, and the interface as it stands
Produces: the before's captures, the kept list and the constraint, as the frame's inputs of a change at careful
Next: frame, under careful, which the owner approves

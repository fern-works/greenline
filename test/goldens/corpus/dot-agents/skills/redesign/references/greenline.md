# Redesign: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where the redesign's work lands and its commands. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

- The before's captures are visuals of the form `capture` under the change's folder in the visual home, `.greenline/visuals/<id>/`, each with its manifest naming the route, the width and the commit; `greenline check interface --url <address> --path <route>` takes them at the three widths.
- The kept list is `.greenline/ledger/<id>/kept.json`: per route its links, fields, handlers, test hooks, script hooks and headings, as the capture command reads them from the page, with any entry you add by hand. At verify the same command takes the after and compares it with the list.
- The constraint stands in the record's `## start` section, with the depth, the routes in scope and the owner's words confirming it.

## The change

The redesign proposes its change at careful, whose record says `interface: "yes, <register>"`; its frame starts from the start section, and its design section deals under the constraint.

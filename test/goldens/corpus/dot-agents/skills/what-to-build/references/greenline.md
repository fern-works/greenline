# What to build: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where what-to-build's work lands in the workspace and the commands it runs, which `SKILL.md` names by their nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

The decisions book is `.greenline/DECISIONS.md`; `greenline status` lists the open changes, their findings and the last finished ones; the finished plans' specs are the `spec.md` files under `.greenline/plans/`.

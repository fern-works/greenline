# Verify: the practice's mechanics

This page holds verify at each mode's depth and where its work lands: the section, the fresh verifier's brief, the recorder's commands, and the working folder and evidence paths `SKILL.md` names by their nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the mode reaches verify, and when AGENTS.md's index sends you here; a fresh verifier reads it with `SKILL.md`.

## The depths

- **Inline, at quick.** The builder verifies the change's claim by `SKILL.md`'s workflow and writes the change record's verify section, in `.greenline/ledger/<id>.md`, as one line: the claim, the check that shows it, and the verdict.
- **Written, at regular, and where the harness has no delegation.** The builder verifies every item of the acceptance by `SKILL.md`'s workflow. The verify section holds each item with its verdict and its evidence: the check by its command, the baseline, the treatment and the comparison. No fresh verifier is started; where the harness has no delegation the section says so.
- **Independent, at careful.** One fresh agent verifies, started through the harness's delegation and inheriting none of the builder's conversation. It is started with the path `greenline brief --change <id> --step verify --round 1` prints, and nothing else: the one brief every fresh agent gets (`.greenline/ledger/README.md`, "The brief and the relay note"), which the command writes from the record to `.greenline/ledger/<id>/brief-verify-1.md`, the change record's front matter, its acceptance, the change's id, the path of its report, `.greenline/ledger/<id>/verify-1.md`, its scratch folder, `.greenline/tmp/<id>/verify-1/`, by its absolute path, and this skill to work by. The builder never writes, opens, copies, diffs or derives a brief file. Verify adds nothing to it: never the builder's other sections or its diagnosis, which are the builder's claims. Every working file it writes goes in its scratch folder or a claim's folder, never the host's temporary directory, a working file there a finding, and it runs each check with `--step verify --round <n>`, which sets `TMPDIR` to its scratch folder. It verifies every item by this skill, starts no agent of its own, and writes its report there; the verify section cites it, item by item. A verifier whose start fails stops the step for the owner, as review's references page says of every fresh agent. The step waits for the report, and no other agent runs beside it.

At the independent depth the agent's verdicts stand as it gave them: the builder records each item with its verdict in the verify section and never re-judges one. A `NOT VERIFIED` item goes back to the build step in verify's own fix pass, the fix rounds the mode gives this step apart from review's; where the mode gives none, or its rounds are spent, the item stays `NOT VERIFIED` in the section and goes to the owner. A fix made after verify is not reviewed again; the owner's word may ask for a second review. An `INCONCLUSIVE` item stays an explicit limit of the proof.

At careful, where the change touches code a verification of the repository covers, a model checker's, a prover's or a code-level verifier's check whose note names its covered files and its sync commit, the fresh verifier reruns that check and records its verdict by it: a failure is `NOT VERIFIED`, and a pass on a verification whose covered code changed after its sync commit is `INCONCLUSIVE`, never `VERIFIED`, until the note describes the code again. The verifier edits no verification: bringing one back in line is verify-formally's upkeep, in verify's fix pass or in a change of its own.

## Recording checks and probes

Run each check through `greenline check -- <command>`, which takes the open change and the step its record is in and names that step beside the facts, or with both named, `greenline check --change <id> --step verify -- <command>`, a fresh verifier adding the `--round <n>` its brief names, as the block gives a fresh agent and wherever the step it names is another, so that the change's checks record it with the commit and the tree id it ran on; a check already recorded on the same tree id is cited from them. `greenline status --change <id>` lists the change's commits. The change is `done` only when the latest run of each check is green. A probe runs through `greenline check` too, a baseline's command run in its copy, such as `greenline check --change <id> --step verify -- npm --prefix .greenline/tmp/<id>/verify/<claim-slug>/baseline test`, and is then marked with `greenline check --change <id> --probe <fact>`, which leaves it out of the change's checks; a fresh verifier does the same.

The change's working folder, `<working folder>` in this skill's pages, is `.greenline/tmp/<id>/`: a claim's artifacts sit under `.greenline/tmp/<id>/verify/<claim-slug>/`, its baseline's copy in its `baseline/` folder, and the captures of the control pages under `.greenline/tmp/<id>/control-cli/` and `.greenline/tmp/<id>/control-ui/`. The claim's evidence, what a verdict relies on, is promoted to `.greenline/ledger/<id>/verify/<claim-slug>/` before the verdict is written.

## An interface change

On a change whose record says `interface: yes`, read design's capture page and run `greenline check interface --url <address> --path <route>` for each route the change touched: it renders the page at 1440, 768 and 390, runs the render rules, keeps the captures as visuals under `.greenline/visuals/<id>/` and updates `.greenline/ledger/interface/paths.json`. On a redesign the same command checks the after against the kept list in `.greenline/ledger/<id>/kept.json`. Give it the app's own address, never the viewer's, which the check refuses; a line `- app <origin>` in the record's `## Interface` section lets `--path` come alone. Where the capture cannot be made, no browser, a page that does not load in time or nothing answering at the address, the check's fact keeps the limit with its cause, the address tried and the time, the command prints it, and the floor stands as checked statically: write it for the owner in the record's `## Interface` section as `- limit <route>: <what was not captured, in your words>`, and the verify section names it too. The change is `done` only once each route it touched carries its capture, or its limit with that line beside it: the commit that sets `done` is refused before it, words with no limit behind them settle nothing, and `greenline status` names the routes still owed and each limit the record does not name yet.

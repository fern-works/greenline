# Show me: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where show-me's work lands in the workspace and the commands it runs, which `SKILL.md` names by their nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

A picture or a view the owner asks to keep is drawn by a fresh agent under the change's working files, `.greenline/tmp/<id>/`, by the brief and the form `.greenline/WORK.md` gives ("Durable visuals"), and kept with `greenline picture keep <file> --change <id>`, which writes it and its manifest `<slug>.visual.json` under `.greenline/visuals/<id>/` together, the owner's words its `ask`; every other write into the change's folder of the visual home is refused. The surface's `renders` fact is the status line's `surface:` line; `greenline status` prints the running surface's row. The count reads every text picture, a box drawing by its boxes, a tree by its lines, a sequence by its lifelines and a drawing of labels joined by arrows by its labels, and a mermaid picture by its nodes; pseudocode carries no drawing glyphs and is never counted. Where the harness hands the hooks the reply's text, the next turn's status line names a picture past twelve nodes or 72 columns, `picture: one past its cap at turn <n>`; a turn that changed code and drew no shape, `picture: the turn changed <n> files and drew no shape`; a second offer in a change, `offer: a second picture offer in this change`; and an offer of a picture status names drifting, stale or deleted, `offer: the offer at turn <n> remakes <id>`; each a notice, never a refusal, and elsewhere these words hold alone. The offers are counted per change in a working file under `.greenline/tmp/sessions/`, never in the record, and no turn's end is held for a picture. Before a durable picture is drawn, its brief stands at `.greenline/tmp/<id>/pictures/<slug>.brief.md`, one line each, `- question:`, `- form:`, `- nodes:`, `- finish:` (text, branded SVG or interactive), `- palette:` and `- reader:`; `greenline picture keep` reads it into the manifest's `brief`, refuses one that does not read, and `greenline status` names a kept picture without one (GL0606).

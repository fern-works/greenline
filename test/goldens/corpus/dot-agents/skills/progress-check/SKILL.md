# Progress Check

progress-check is the habit for long work with no change open: a survey, a
long diagnosis, delegated agents running one after another outside a change's
steps. Inside a change the block's reply section owns progress: its progress
line counts the change's steps, and a line says when a step ends. This habit
never fires on a short answer or on work whose only honest states are started
and finished, and its cadence is judgment, never spam. It
writes no record of its own: progress stays in the conversation, and an
estimated clock is an unmeasured claim this habit never makes.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the first update, read [method.md](method.md): a change of reporting the owner asks for, the mode, reporting progress and persisting a preference.

Durable output: none. Applied on its own cadence while long work runs, and the moment the owner asks to change how or how often you report, before any preference is saved anywhere.

# Prose

prose is the habit for the occasions the block's reply section leaves to it: a document, a README, an RFC, a PR description or a commit message, written by [documents.md](documents.md); an answer the owner contradicts; and a reply that did not land. It writes no record of its own, applies on your own judgment and is never announced. The shape of every reply, the answer first, the measurement in place of the adjective and the plain words, is the reply section of AGENTS.md, which loads on every turn. Before prose lands in a document, cut the tells [tells.md](tells.md) catalogs.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before an answer the owner contradicts or a reply that did not land, read [method.md](method.md): the answer's evidence and the re-pitch.

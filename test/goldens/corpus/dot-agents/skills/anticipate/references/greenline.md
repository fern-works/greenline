# Anticipate: the practice's mechanics

This page holds anticipate at each mode's depth, the careful mode's premise review, and where its work lands. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the mode reaches anticipate, and when AGENTS.md's index sends you here.

## The depths

- **None, at quick and regular.** The mode skips the anticipate step, and its section holds the one line `none at <mode>`.
- **Independent, at careful: the premise review.** One fresh agent attacks the claims the build rests on before any code, `SKILL.md`'s cycle running once on each non-trivial claim with that agent as its fresh reading. At the independent depth the fresh reading is one agent, started through the harness's delegation with the one brief every fresh agent gets (`.greenline/ledger/README.md`, "The brief and the relay note"), written to `.greenline/tmp/<id>/brief-anticipate-1.md` as well: the change record's front matter, its acceptance, the change's id, the path of its report, `.greenline/ledger/<id>/anticipate-1.md`, and this skill to work by, whose adversarial prompt it runs; then anticipate's extras, ARTIFACT and CONTRACT, and never the CLAIM. Nothing else. It starts no agent of its own. It writes its report there, and the anticipate section cites it. The step waits for the report, and no other agent runs beside it. The report has four parts, each under its own heading: `## Claims tried`, each property of the contract the agent tested the artifact against; `## Held`, each claim that survived, with what it checked; `## Broke`, each claim that failed, with the place in the artifact, the contract line it breaks and the input or state that breaks it; and `## Limits`, what it could not test and why. A round that breaks nothing says so under its Broke heading and still states its limits. Step 4 reconciles what broke. The anticipate section holds the claims attacked, each finding with its class and what was done about it, citing the report.
- **Written, where the harness has no delegation, and when anticipate is asked for by name.** Run the cycle once on each non-trivial claim in the self-questioning form. The anticipate section, in `.greenline/ledger/<id>.md`, holds the claims attacked, each finding with its class and what was done about it, and the note that the reading was degraded.

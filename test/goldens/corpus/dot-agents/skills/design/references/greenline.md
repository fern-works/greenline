# Design: the practice's mechanics

This page holds design at each mode's depth and where its work lands: the fact that decides whether it runs, the section, the home's files, the pick and the commands. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the mode reaches design, and when AGENTS.md's index sends you here.

## Whether it runs

Design runs only where the change's record says `interface: yes`, or `interface: "yes, <register>"` with `site` or `app`, the fact the proposal states wherever `.greenline/manifest.json` names interface roots. Where the record says `interface: no`, or the workspace names no interface, the design section of `.greenline/ledger/<id>.md` is the one line `none, no interface`, and none of this skill's words are read. A write under an interface root while the record says no is refused by the harness hook, naming the proposal: state the fact in the record and wait for the owner's yes. At the first request that touches a page or a screen, the proposal states the fact, and where `.greenline/manifest.json` holds no `interface` block, it proposes the setting in the owner's words before any build: the folders the pages live under and whether the check holds each change, written into `.greenline/manifest.json`'s `interface` block on the owner's yes. A request to change how a working page looks while it keeps what it does, denser, cleaner or like another product, is a redesign: the start `redesign` runs first, its before capture and its kept list written before any edit.

## The depths

- **Inline, at quick.** One line: the lock read and worked within, such as `lock read: DESIGN.md; components: form`, or the direction the owner named.
- **Written, at regular.** The section: the `components:` line; the lock read, or the ask's four answers; the three directions dealt or composed, each with its catalog ids where the catalog dealt it; the owner's pick in the owner's words; and the lock written, naming DESIGN.md's tokens and the rulings it changed. Where nothing locks the direction, deal the three and show them, then wait for the owner's pick before any build: a direction you compose is never the owner's, and a likeness the request names, such as "like Linear", names a direction to deal, never a pick.
- **Independent, at careful, one fresh agent.** One fresh agent composes the three directions, or prototypes the pick on the real route, from the one brief every fresh agent gets (`.greenline/ledger/README.md`, "The brief and the relay note"), the ask's answers and the lock as its extra. Its report is `.greenline/ledger/<id>/design-1.md`, its first line the nonce of its brief, `<!-- brief nonce <hex> -->`, the brief `greenline brief --change <id> --step design --round 1 --extras "<the ask's answers and the lock>"` writes and whose path starts the agent; the section cites it and records the owner's pick, once the three are shown and the owner has picked, as at regular. A start that fails stops the step for the owner, as review's references page says; where the session has no delegation tool at all, you write the section yourself, naming the limit.

## The home

- **DESIGN.md**, at the workspace's root, and at a root whose register differs, the nearest file governing a path; its format, the tokens in the front matter and the eight sections, is on [home.md](home.md).
- **The rulings**, the `## Interface` section of `.greenline/DECISIONS.md`, one dash line per ruling: `register: app at src`, one per root; `platform: web`; `anti-reference: <text>`; `direction: <entry>, catalog <pin>, <date>`; `shell: <text>`; `waiver: <rule>: <reason>`; `bar: <text>`; `locked: <section>, <section>`.
- **The interface ledger home**, `.greenline/ledger/interface/`: `survey.json`, the survey's sheet, one source per row, and `paths.json`, the index of each address's register, last capture and scores.

DESIGN.md and the ledger home are the record's own writes: the harness hook admits them before the owner's yes. The lock changes them only on the owner's pick, and `.greenline/manifest.json`'s `interface` block only on the owner's confirmation of the detected facts.

## The pick

Where cards render, the owner picks in the round's cards. Where they do not, the deal view of `greenline inspect` shows the three directions side by side, and its pick button writes `.greenline/ledger/<id>/design-pick.json`; the status line prints the pick at the next turn, and you write it into the section and the lock.

## The check and its waivers

Where `.greenline/manifest.json`'s `interface` block sets `check` to true, a change whose record says `interface: yes` is held to the check `interface` as one of its declared checks, with no declaration of yours; build and verify run it through `greenline check -- greenline check interface`, which records each run. Its facts list the findings by tier: a floor finding makes the run red and holds `done` as any red check does, and only the owner rules on it: put a floor finding the check or you name to the owner on a card before verify passes, and write their answer in the change record's `## Interface` section, `- floor <rule> at <where>: fixed` or `- floor <rule> at <where>: waived, "<their words>"`, the waiver clearing that rule's floor for this change alone, while git's hooks refuse `done` with a named floor finding that has no ruling, and the kept list's and the redesign's findings are never waived; a standing waiver never clears a floor; a reflex finding is cleared by a line in the record's build section, `waived: <rule>: <reason>; the owner's yes: "<the owner's words>"`, or by a standing waiver in the rulings; a finish finding is listed and never blocks. A reflex finding you read as a false alarm is put to the owner once per workspace for its rule, on one card in the owner's terms and never by the rule's name, yes recommended: "The style check flags the seven lines that define your colours, a false alarm. Shall I tell it to ignore them for this site?" The owner's yes is the clearance, written as the standing waiver `waiver: <rule>: <what it protects>` in the rulings, so no later change asks again. Never tell the owner to leave a file of theirs alone. The harness hook runs the same rules on each interface file you write, through a shell command too, and returns their findings by tier to you at once, the counts where it finds none, or at the next turn where the harness takes no context after a tool. A change whose record says a redesign holds the check red, `redesign-evidence`, until the redesign start was loaded, a before capture of each page it reshapes stands and the kept list that before wrote stands. A server's template, under a `templates` or `views` folder or in a template language's extension, has no address in its path: name the address the app serves it at in the record's `## Interface` section, `- page <address>: <path>`, and the check names a template no page line names and holds nothing on it. Those static runs never settle the render: `done` waits until each route the change touched carries a capture at 1440, 768 and 390, or the limit the engine named with the record's `- limit <route>: <your words>` line beside it in its `## Interface` section, from `greenline check interface --url <address> --path <route>` at verify, the app's own address and never the viewer's, and on a platform no browser renders each screen the change touched carries the limit `greenline check interface --path <screen>` records, and `greenline status` names the routes still owed.

## Commands

- `greenline status` prints the interface: the facets in play, DESIGN.md read or the home offered, and the drift between the code and DESIGN.md.
- `greenline check interface` checks the interface files the change touched against the registry's rules, by tier; at verify, `greenline check interface --url <address> --path <route>` renders and captures each route the change touched, and [capture.md](capture.md) says what it runs.
- `greenline mark list` prints the owner's open marks on captures; [marks.md](marks.md) says what to do with them.

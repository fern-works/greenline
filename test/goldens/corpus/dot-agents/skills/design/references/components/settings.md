# Settings

Read this page when the change builds or changes a settings page.

- **Anatomy.** Each section is a row: a label column of about 450 pixels, its title and a short muted description, beside a panel of about 700 pixels; sections are split by a one pixel rule with room above and below.
- **Words.** A toggle is labelled for its on state; a mention of another setting links to it; the tone is neutral and brief.
- **Saving.** One save model per view, either saving as you go with a quiet "Saved", or one save button per panel; each area has its own view; a small preview shows what a setting produces.
- **Destructive actions.** They sit last, in their own section, and confirm by naming the object.
- **Gated features.** A feature the plan does not include shows a slim tinted strip with its upgrade action, and the locked panel stays readable.
- **Responsive collapse.** On a narrow screen the label column stacks above its panel.
- **Native.** A Mac app's settings live in a settings window with tabs and no pushed navigation; iOS uses inset grouped lists; settings are searchable and can be reset.

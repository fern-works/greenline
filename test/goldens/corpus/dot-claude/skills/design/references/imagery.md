# Imagery

Read this page when a page needs pictures, illustration or icons. Real material comes first, and nothing is invented.

## The order of preference

1. The owner's own pictures: ask, and look in the repository.
2. Real pictures of the subject's world from open collections, credited.
3. Drawings of the subject's own things, made in code.
4. Type and colour alone, chosen on purpose.

A generated picture and a hotlinked stock photograph are never used. Tell the deal truthfully what material exists, so no direction needs pictures the product does not have.

## Finding pictures

Search the subject's own world, never its category: its place, its maps and materials, the history of its trade, museum objects of its kind, scientific plates; use local words with a medium or a decade, two or three queries per idea. A good find would not fit a competitor's page, reads at the crop it needs, is at least twice the shown width when full-bleed, belongs to one family with the page's other pictures, and carries no watermark, burned-in text, frame or recognisable living person.

## Honesty and licences

- A found picture never stands in for the owner's product, rooms, people, team or customers, and a caption never ties it to the owner.
- Only CC0, public domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA pictures are used; a noncommercial or no-derivatives licence is out, and an altered CC BY-SA picture keeps its licence.
- Every picture's source, creator, licence and page are recorded; one credit line at body size in the footer names the pictures in use, with links.
- Pictures are downloaded into the project at sizes that fit their place, never hotlinked.

## Drawing

Draw the subject's own things, never decoration, in one graphic language and the palette's real colours, with three levels of detail, large shapes, middle shapes and small details. Say near the drawings that they stand in until photographs exist. To show software, build a real fragment at true size with values that add up, and no drawn browser bar or device frame; a real screenshot from the owner is shown large and uncropped at least once.

## Placement

Use a small fixed set of ratios, go large and crop with intent; one radius, no frames and no shadows; one treatment for the whole page, untouched, greyscale, multiplied on a field or darkened. Text over a picture sits on a neutral scrim of 40 to 55 percent black or on a calm region of the picture. Every picture has a ground colour behind it while it loads. With type and colour alone, leave no empty box where a picture will go, no icon in a square, no abstract gradient and no emoji standing in for an icon.

## The checked rules

The registry's rules for this page's facets, each with its tier; the interface check runs them on the files you write, and a reviewer cites them by their ids.

- `no-fake-faces`, floor: No generated or placeholder faces stand in for real people (pravatar, dicebear, randomuser and the like).
- `image-loads`, floor: Every visible image loads.
- `style-emoji-icon`, reflex: An emoji standing in for an icon is a style default, waivable.
- `style-drawn-chrome`, reflex: A hand-drawn browser bar or device frame is a style default, waivable.

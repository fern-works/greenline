# Pricing

Read this page when the change builds or changes a pricing section or a plan's usage.

- **Anatomy.** Plans in a table with a period switch where there are periods, the main action beside each price, a comparison table below where plans differ in many ways, and the questions people ask near the prices.
- **Honesty.** No price, discount or figure is invented; where none is given, the section waits for the owner. At least one real reversal of risk is shown, such as a trial, cancelling at any time or a guarantee, and only one the owner offers.
- **Type.** Prices use tabular figures on the page's anchor lines; the heading is a plain "Pricing", never a small tracked label above it.
- **Consent.** No box is checked for the person, and structured data never claims a price the page does not show.
- **In an app.** A quota view leads with a usage bar at the true ratio, labelled at both ends.
- **Responsive collapse.** On a phone plans stack, the recommended one first, and the comparison table scrolls inside its own frame.

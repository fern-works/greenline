# Deep modules: the practice's mechanics

This page holds where deep-modules's work lands in the workspace and the commands it runs, which `SKILL.md` names by their nouns. Read it with `SKILL.md` when the work lands in the workspace.

## Where its work lands

A design agent of DESIGN-IT-TWICE.md gets the one brief every fresh agent gets, which the ledger guide states (`.greenline/ledger/README.md`, "The brief and the relay note"), and writes its design as its report, `.greenline/ledger/<id>/<step>-<n>.md`, named for the step the design serves.

Use when the owner asks for the work planned in tickets, or for a huge chunk of work spanning several sessions."

wayfinder is the start for a decision space too large for one session, spanning more than one context: it charts that space as the map of a plan directory, and works it down until the way is clear. A single design that needs stress-testing is one change's frame; a settled compact change needs no map.

Greenline's paths and commands for this skill stand on its references page, [references/greenline.md](references/greenline.md): read it when the work lands in the workspace.

Before the map is charted, read [method.md](method.md): its planning change, the destination, plan don't do, the map and its tickets, the fog of war, charting and working through the map.

## Handoff

Consumes: a loose idea too big for one session; the planning change's record and its plan directory's existing decisions.md when one exists
Produces: the plan directory's map.md, status draft while charting and complete when the way is clear; each resolved ticket's answer in the plan directory's decisions.md or a note under its research/; the frame section of the planning change's record citing the map
Next: plan, the planning change's step once the way is clear, which reads the resolved decisions into the plan directory's spec.md and tickets.md
